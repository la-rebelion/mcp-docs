# HAPI v1.2 Private Beta

**Private Beta available by request:** [request a demo](https://mcp.com.ai/request-demo).

We are inviting teams that want to make their MCP experiences more helpful,
predictable, and safe. The Private Beta focuses on Capability Graph, Capability
Planning, and the optional Enterprise classifier powered by Jev from TypeSafe.

## A strong fit for the beta

Even the most capable AI model, or MCP client, can behave erratically when a workflow lacks deterministic guidance. This is often not an MCP problem; it is a workflow-design problem. The beta is a strong fit for teams whose agents struggle with missed steps, incorrect tool usage, ambiguous decisions, or inconsistent outcomes because the required information, sequencing, authorization, and confirmation rules are not explicit.

The beta is especially useful if your team has:

- an OpenAPI API with many tools or overlapping operations;
- an MCP client that needs better tool selection than a flat list provides;
- a workflow where missing information and confirmation matter;
- a product team ready to test realistic examples and share feedback.

Healthcare scheduling, account support, commerce operations, travel changes,
and internal service workflows are all strong candidates.

## What we will explore together

- A Capability Graph for the operations that matter most.
- Deterministic tool discovery and authorization-aware filtering.
- Planning scenarios that reveal “ready,” “missing information,” confirmation,
  and “already complete” states.
- Optional Jev-based TypeSafe classification for nuanced requests and tool
  ranking.
- The boundaries that should remain deterministic and under your team’s
  control.

## What stays under your control

Your API contract, authorization model, and business policies remain yours.
Jev is an optional semantic classifier, not an autonomous actor. Capability
Planning is read-only; it prepares a proposed path and does not change data.

If the opportunity sounds relevant, [request a demo](https://mcp.com.ai/request-demo)
and tell us about the API, the MCP client, and one workflow you would like an
assistant to guide more reliably.
