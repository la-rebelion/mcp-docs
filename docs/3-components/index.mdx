## In your IDE: OrcA

[**OrcA**](./orca/index.md) (Orchestrator for APIs and Agents) is the IDE component of the stack. It is a [VS Code extension](../10-integrations/vscode/index.md) that turns your OpenAPI and Arazzo contracts into MCP servers: it previews their MCP tools, runs them with the HAPI CLI, deploys them to runMCP, and connects them to your AI assistant.
